Privacy

01

ARCHIVE

ARCHIVE is an independent curated platform dedicated to places shaped by architecture.

For privacy-related enquiries, please use the Contact page.

02

Analytics

If you consent to analytics, ARCHIVE uses Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use the website and to measure interactions such as outbound booking clicks.

Analytics is not loaded if you reject analytics cookies.

03

Contact and submissions

When you use the Contact or Submit a place forms, the information that you provide, such as your name, email address and message, is processed for the purpose of responding to your enquiry or reviewing a submission.

04

External links

ARCHIVE contains links to third-party websites, including architecture websites and booking platforms. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave ARCHIVE.

05

Your choices

You can change your analytics preference at any time through Cookie settings in the footer.
