ABOUT ARCHIVE

ARCHIVE is a curated collection of places where architecture can be experienced rather than simply observed.

The archive brings together hotels, houses, restaurants, cultural spaces and other places selected for their architectural significance.

Projects can be explored through architects, destinations, typologies and geography, creating an alternative way of discovering architecture through travel.

01

Architecture first

Every place is selected primarily for the quality and relevance of its architecture.

02

Curated, not exhaustive

ARCHIVE is a selection rather than a directory. Inclusion depends on architectural interest, not quantity.

03

Experience the work

The focus is on places that can be visited, inhabited, used or experienced directly.
